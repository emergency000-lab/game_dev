Rath Yatra: Three Chariots

Pull the sacred ropes through a festive Puri-style avenue. Keep pace with the three animated raths, collect flower offerings, and avoid crowd gaps and barricades.

How to play

  • ←→ or swipe sideways to change rope lanes.
  • ↑ or swipe up to jump over flower baskets.
  • ↓ or swipe down to duck under low festival banners.
  • Space starts or restarts the procession.
