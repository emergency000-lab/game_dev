Controls & Accessibility

  • ←→ or swipe left/right: move the Rath between lanes.
  • Swipe up: Speed Boost • swipe down: Brake / Slide • double tap: Divine Power.
  • Long press: Temple Bell slow motion ability.
  • Devotees board automatically when the Rath reaches their boarding area.
  • P: pause • H: high contrast • M: mute ambience.
  • Large touch buttons, reduced-motion particles, and easy mode are available.
